With features like improved **Tracking Protection** and revamped **tab management**, Firefox is not just keeping up with the times—it’s setting the pace.

Tracking Protection in Firefox 142 is a transformative feature. By blocking certain trackers, it not only fortifies user privacy but also improves **page load times**. The introduction of an **exceptions feature** means users can now manually or automatically exclude sites that might otherwise break under strict tracking protocols. This feature applies only to the [strict level of Tracking Protection](https://www.ghacks.net/2025/08/19/firefox-142-ships-with-tracking-protection-tab-groups-and-extensions-improvements/), ensuring that users can maintain their desired level of privacy while enjoying their favorite sites. Additionally, this update addresses several [security vulnerabilities](https://aphnetworks.com/news/30630-firefox-142-rolls-out-its-pocket-replacement-plus-more-ai-features), further enhancing the overall safety of users while browsing.

> Firefox 142 revolutionizes privacy with enhanced Tracking Protection, blocking trackers while boosting page load speeds and allowing site exceptions for seamless browsing.

Imagine toggling a setting and, like magic, your favourite site is back up and running without sacrificing the protection you crave. Configuration is straightforward in the **about:preferences#privacy** section, ensuring users can fine-tune their experience with ease. “It’s about finding the sweet spot between privacy and functionality,” one tech analyst remarked, highlighting how critical this balance is in today’s web environment.

The tab management improvements are no less impressive. Firefox 142’s **Tab Groups** feature now allows users to maintain visibility of the **active tab** even when the group is collapsed. This means that as other tabs slip into the background, the one you need remains front and centre.

It’s like having a loyal companion that refuses to let you lose track of what’s important. With a consistent active tab highlight, manoeuvring through multiple tabs becomes a breeze, ensuring that users can focus on what matters most without the headache of confusion.

Moreover, the **New Tab page** has undergone a significant transformation, replacing Pocket with a **curated news overview** tailored to individual interests. Users can select or deselect topics to create a **personalised news feed**, showcasing Mozilla’s commitment to user engagement.

“This shift from Pocket reflects a keen understanding of user preferences,” notes a digital media expert.
