Common Keyboard Problems. Encountering problems with your keyboard? Here are some of the most common causes as well as what you can do to fix them. [common mouse hardware issues](/list-of-common-mouse-problems/) can greatly affect your productivity and overall experience with your computer. From unresponsive buttons to erratic cursor movements, these problems can often be traced back to simple fixes. Regular maintenance and proper troubleshooting techniques can help identify and resolve these frustrating issues efficiently. When dealing with [slow computer performance indicators](/common-signals-your-computer-is-in-need-of-computer-maintenance/), it’s essential to check for background applications that may be consuming excessive resources. Additionally, ensuring that your hardware is properly updated and that your software is optimized can significantly enhance your computer’s responsiveness. Taking these proactive steps can lead to a smoother and more efficient user experience.

• **One of the causes of keyboard problems is an improper connection.** Improper connection can be fixed by reconnecting your keyboard then restarting your computer.

![](https://www.m.eet.com/media/1117292/ps2-keyboard.jpg)

• **If the keyboard still doesn’t work after you reconnect it, then connection is not the problem.** Turn off your computer, unplug the keyboard, and then check for bent or broken pins. Straighten the bent pins gently with the help of a ball point pen or a pencil. If the pins are broken, it’s time to get a replacement.

![](https://jpdiv.files.wordpress.com/2011/02/ing0197prcsd.jpg)

• **Another method you can try is updating your device driver.** Visit the manufacturer’s site for the latest drivers available for your keyboard maker and model.

![](https://www.community.emerson.com/embeddedimages/CAC+driver+win+update.jpg)

• **While you’re at the website, see if there are software updates available for your keyboard.** If there are any, install them as well.

![](https://www.www.driverboost.com/driver-tutorial-center/instruction-images/acer/acer_driver_download_03.png)

• **If you are using a multimedia type keyboard, be sure to remove the software before installing any new drivers for this type of keyboard.** 

![](https://www.iogear.com/product_images/web/hi/GKM561R_0.jpg)
