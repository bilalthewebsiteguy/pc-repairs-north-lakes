## **The iPhone 11 Camera and Other Features**

Same with iPhone XR, iPhone 11 also features the “Liquid Retina HD Display”, a 6.1-inch all-screen LCD Multi-Touch display with IPS Technology. It also features a 1400:1 contrast ratio, 625 nits max brightness, and a 1792-by-828-pixel resolution at 326 PPI. The phone also has the true tone support feature which allows the phone to adjusts to its color temperature based on the light of its current surroundings and the wide color display for real-life color display on your phone. 

The iPhone 11 also comes with a dual 12MP ultra-wide and wide cameras. Its set-up features the standard wide-angle camera and the latest ultra-wide-angle camera that comes with a 120-degree field of view. This type of camera, according to Apple, captures four times much better compared to previous cameras and is good for taking landscape shots, architectural photos, tight shots, and a whole lot more. This feature is way much better compared to the iPhone XR which only supports shots in portrait mode. The phone also has the new night mode feature which was designed to use the latest camera features to capture bright and clear photos even in very low-lighting conditions.  

The smartphone’s battery life is also much better compared to the previous iPhone XR as it lasts 1 more hour compared to its predecessor. Moreover, the battery of the iPhone 11 can support at least 65 hours of audio playback, up to 10 hours of streamed video playback, and up to 17 hours of video playback. In addition, the battery has a fast-charged capability which can make a battery charged half-full for only 30 minutes. It also has a wireless-charging capability using Qi chargers or you can simply charge it via USB to your computer or on your power adapter.
