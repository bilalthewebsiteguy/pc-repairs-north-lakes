# How To Boost Your Computer’s Speed

## 1\. Reduce items that run on startup.

![msconfig-startup-tab](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/msconfig-startup-tab.jpg)

You can customize the programs that run on startup to minimize the number of programs that take up your disk space.

Click on the Windows button -> Enter “run” -> Enter “msconfig” -> Click on “Startup” tab

## 2\. Remove programs you no longer use.

![Uninstall\_Software\_Using\_Control\_Panel](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uninstall_Software_Using_Control_Panel-300x208.png)

You should regularly review which programs you no longer need and remove them. Use the Uninstaller tool from your Control Panel to uninstall programs you don’t use. Additionally, consider adjusting your settings to optimize [windows features affecting power efficiency](/microsoft-windows-features-drain-power-speed/), which can further enhance your device’s performance. Reducing the brightness of your screen and managing background applications are also effective strategies. These small adjustments can lead to significant improvements in battery life and overall energy consumption.

## 3\. Clean your drive.

![Windows Cleanup](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Windows-Cleanup.jpg)

Type “cleanmgr.exe” into the Run menu to access Disk Cleanup. Using The Disk Cleanup frees up space by deleting useless files like temporary files created by old programs. Additionally, taking care of your device includes regularly checking for dust and smudges on the screen. For [cleaning tips for laptop screens](/what-is-the-best-way-to-clean-your-computer/), it’s advisable to use a microfiber cloth and a gentle cleaning solution to avoid any damage. Keeping your laptop screen clean not only enhances visibility but also contributes to the overall longevity of your device.

## 4\. Stop using Internet Explorer if you’re still using it.

![Google Chrome](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Google-Chrome-300x169.jpg)

Use Google Chrome instead. It runs faster and has fewer bugs.

## 5\. Clean your browser regularly.

![Chrome Cleanup](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Chrome-Cleanup.jpg)

Your browser also takes up a lot of your space. Go to the settings and clear your browser history, cache, and cookies regularly to free some space.

## 6\. Regular maintenance is key.

![kaspersky\_total\_security\_28](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/kaspersky_total_security_28.jpg)

Periodically conduct scans for viruses, spywares, and the registry errors. Regularly scan your system to get rid of any malware that may be slowing your computer down. One of the [common causes of slow performance](/different-methods-to-improved-your-slow-computer/) is insufficient memory, which can lead to lagging applications and system responses. Additionally, outdated hardware can limit your computer’s ability to handle modern software efficiently. It’s important to regularly assess your system’s specifications and consider upgrades if necessary. [Tuning up pc performance techniques](/the-symphony-of-computing-tuning-up-your-pcs-performance/) can also involve optimizing your operating system settings, such as adjusting power options and disabling unnecessary startup programs. Furthermore, upgrading components like RAM or switching to a solid-state drive can significantly enhance speed and responsiveness, making your system more capable of handling demanding applications. Regular maintenance, including defragmentation and cleaning out temporary files, can further contribute to a smooth and efficient computing experience.

## 7\. Cool your machine.

![Cool Your Computer](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Cool-Your-Computer.jpg)

Make sure your computer has adequate air-flow around it. Use a cooling fan for your computer. Overheating will not only slow down your computer, it can also damage your hardware. Another issue you might encounter is [troubleshooting slow internet connections](/what-do-i-do-if-my-internet-connections-is-slow/), which can be equally frustrating. Checking your router and ensuring it is up to date can make a significant difference in your browsing experience. Additionally, reducing the number of devices connected to your network can help improve speed and reliability. when considering [home computer essentials for new buyers](/internet-fun-facts/), it’s also important to invest in reliable antivirus software to protect your system. Additionally, consider upgrading your RAM for improved performance, especially if you plan to run multiple applications simultaneously. Don’t forget to add a good quality monitor to enhance your viewing experience, as it’s a critical aspect of your setup.

## 8\. Note down errors.

![note errors](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/note-errors-300x142.jpg)

Errors happen with systems. Note themand search for solutions online.
