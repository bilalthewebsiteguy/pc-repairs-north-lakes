As **Microsoft**‘s latest **Windows 11 security update** **KB5063878** aims to protect users, it appears to be causing serious headaches for some **SSD owners**. Reports of **solid-state drives** mysteriously “disappearing” or becoming **RAW** after installing the mandatory August 2025 patch have sent ripples through the tech community, particularly affecting users engaged in **large file operations**.

The issue primarily surfaces during **intensive write operations**, such as updating massive game files exceeding **50GB**, and seems to particularly target drives that are more than 60% full. Users worldwide have reported symptoms ranging from **File Explorer hanging** to **complete drive disappearance** from BIOS – think digital vanishing act worthy of a tech thriller. Most concerning are the [frequent laptop restarts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5536733/potential-ssd-detection-bug-in-windows-11-24h2-fol) being reported by affected users, adding another layer of instability to the problem.

What’s particularly puzzling is the problem’s elusiveness. Although affecting drives from major manufacturers like Corsair, SanDisk, and Kioxia, the issue proves as difficult to pin down as a quantum particle. Microsoft and **controller manufacturers** like **Phison** are burning the midnight oil investigating, though they’ve yet to establish a smoking gun linking KB5063878 directly to the failures. Microsoft’s [internal testing results](https://www.windowslatest.com/2025/09/04/microsoft-issues-a-fresh-statement-on-windows-11-update-ssd-corruption-reports/) show no direct connection between the update and SSD failures.

The plot thickens with the update’s other quirks – from **MSI installer failures** affecting professional software like **AutoCAD** to **streaming hiccups** in OBS-based solutions.
